TO MAKE EMAILS ARRIVE (one-time)
  1. Upload v53. Open https://gutsaga.tech/contact/ and send one message.
  2. FormSubmit sends an ACTIVATION email to info@gutsaga.tech. The mailbox is
     at mailbox.org — check Inbox AND the Junk / Spam folder (and wait a few
     minutes: mailbox.org may delay first mails from a new sender).
  3. Click "Activate Form" in that email. From then on every form arrives.
     The message sent before activation is delivered too.
  If no activation email comes at all: send a test mail from Gmail to
  info@gutsaga.tech to confirm the mailbox receives outside mail.
