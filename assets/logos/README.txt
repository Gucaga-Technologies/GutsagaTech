CLIENT LOGOS — drop files here and they appear automatically
=============================================================
The homepage client bar shows: Camelia, Monos Pharma Trade, Tegra State, Alfapharm.

Monos and Tegra already have logo files (in assets/).
Camelia and Alfapharm currently fall back to a clean text wordmark, because we do
not have their logo files yet.

To replace the wordmarks with the real logos, save the files here with EXACTLY
these names — nothing else needs changing:

    assets/logos/camelia.png
    assets/logos/alfapharm.png

PNG or WebP with a transparent background, about 200-400 px wide, works best.
The bar renders every logo at 38 px tall, greyscale, and shows it in full colour
on hover.
