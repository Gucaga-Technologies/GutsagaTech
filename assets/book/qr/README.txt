QR CODES FOR THE BOOK
=====================
Print-ready, black-on-white, high error correction (survives a logo overlay or a
small print size). All three are 1000+ px square PNG — scale them, don't stretch.

  qr-ru-evolution.png  ->  https://gutsaga.tech/ru/evolution/
      THIS IS THE ONE FOR THE BOOK. The Russian page a reader lands on after
      finishing it: the self-check, the four ways to continue, the author page.

  qr-ru-books.png      ->  https://gutsaga.tech/ru/books/
      The Russian library page, for a back cover or a flyer.

  qr-en-book.png       ->  https://gutsaga.tech/book/
      The English page, for international events and LinkedIn.

Print at 25 mm or larger. Keep the white margin around the code.
If a URL ever changes, regenerate rather than redirect — a printed book cannot
be updated.
